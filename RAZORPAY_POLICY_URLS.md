## How to Submit to Razorpay

1. Log in to your Razorpay Dashboard
2. Go to **Settings** → **Website & App Settings**
3. Scroll to **Business Website Details**
4. Add the policy URLs in respective fields:
   - **Privacy Policy URL:** https://v4peace.com/privacy-policy
   - **Terms & Conditions URL:** https://v4peace.com/termsAndConditions
   - **Refund Policy URL:** https://v4peace.com/refund-policy

5. Click **Save**
6. Wait for Razorpay to verify (usually takes a few hours)
